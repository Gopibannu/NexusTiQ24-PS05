STANDARD APARTMENT LEASE CONTRACT

PARTIES AND UNIT:
This Contract is made between Beacon Management LLC ("Lessor") and Robert Smith ("Lessee") for Unit 12C located at 100 Metro Plaza.

LEASE PERIOD:
The lease starts on March 1, 2024, and terminates on February 28, 2025.

MONTHLY RENT:
The agreed monthly rental rate is $2,500.00, due on or before the 1st of each month.

SECURITY DEPOSIT REQUIREMENTS:
Lessee shall submit a refundable security deposit of $5,000.00, representing exactly 2.0 months of base rent, upon signature of this agreement.

TERMINATION NOTIFICATION:
Either party may issue a notice of non-renewal or intent to vacate by delivering sixty (60) days written notice prior to the expiration date.

LESSOR MAINTENANCE DUTIES:
Lessor agrees to keep all central heating, cooling, plumbing, structural components, and major building utilities in proper operating condition. Lessee is obligated to maintain ordinary internal cleanliness and report defects promptly.

SECURITY DEPOSIT REFUND TIMELINE:
Lessor will return the security deposit, alongside a detailed written itemization of any deductions, no later than thirty (30) calendar days after key surrender.

ACCESS TO PREMISES:
Lessor agrees not to enter the unit without delivering forty-eight (48) hours notice, except in emergency situations.

Executed this 1st day of February, 2024.

Lessor: Beacon Management LLC
Lessee: Robert Smith
