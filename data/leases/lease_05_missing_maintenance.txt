RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made between Echo Ridge Properties ("Landlord") and David Miller ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
Tenant leases the residential apartment at 88 Fairview Avenue, Unit 5.

2. TERM & RENT
The term is 12 months starting April 1, 2024. Rent is $2,200.00 monthly, payable on the 1st.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall pay a deposit of $3,300.00 (1.5 months rent) prior to taking possession.

4. NOTICE OF TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this lease upon expiration by delivering thirty (30) days advance written notice.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT RETURN
The security deposit shall be returned within thirty (30) calendar days after lease termination and key surrender, accompanied by an itemized list of deductions.

6. UTILITIES AND OCCUPANCY
Tenant shall register and pay for electric and gas service directly. Max occupancy is restricted to two adults. No unauthorized alterations permitted.

7. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
Landlord reserves the right to enter the unit upon 24 hours written notice to inspect the premises.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by state residential housing regulations.

Signed on March 15, 2024.

Landlord: Echo Ridge Properties
Tenant: David Miller
