RESIDENTIAL APARTMENT LEASE

This Agreement is made on May 1, 2024, between Horizon Properties ("Landlord") and Laura Davis ("Tenant").

1. RENTAL UNIT
Apartment 404, 150 Harbor View Way. Monthly rent is $2,200.00.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall deposit $2,200.00 (1.0 month rent) as security prior to occupancy.

3. LANDLORD MAINTENANCE
Landlord maintains structural framing, roof, HVAC systems, and main water lines. Tenant handles basic domestic upkeep.

4. DEPOSIT RETURN
Security deposit returned within thirty (30) days following move-out inspection.

5. TERMINATION NOTICE PERIOD
Clause 5: Tenant or Landlord may terminate this lease at the expiration of the term by providing at least thirty (30) days prior written notice.

6. ACCESS RIGHTS
Landlord will provide 24 hours notice prior to non-emergency visits.

7. ADDITIONAL OCCUPANCY RULES & VACATING PROCEDURES
Clause 14: Tenant agrees that written notification of intent to vacate must be delivered to Landlord no less than ninety (90) days prior to the expiration date. Failure to provide ninety (90) days notice will result in forfeiture of the security deposit.

Signed: May 1, 2024.
