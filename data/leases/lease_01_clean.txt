RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on January 15, 2024, by and between Apex Property Holdings ("Landlord") and Jane Doe ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND TERM
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the residential property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Suite 4B ("Premises"). The term of this Lease shall be for twelve (12) months, commencing on February 1, 2024, and ending on January 31, 2025.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord base rent in the amount of $2,000.00 per month, payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Prior to occupancy, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $3,000.00 (equal to 1.5 months' rent) as a Security Deposit. The Security Deposit shall be held as security for the faithful performance by Tenant of all terms, covenants, and conditions of this Lease.

4. NOTICE OF TERMINATION AND NON-RENEWAL
Either Landlord or Tenant may terminate or elect not to renew this Lease upon the expiration of the initial term by serving thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Landlord shall be strictly responsible for maintaining the structural integrity of the Premises, including exterior walls, roof, foundations, plumbing, electrical wiring, and HVAC systems. Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition and shall be responsible for minor light bulb replacements and repairs resulting from Tenant negligence.

6. RETURN OF SECURITY DEPOSIT
Within twenty-one (21) calendar days following the expiration of this Lease and surrender of the Premises by Tenant, Landlord shall refund the Security Deposit in full, less any lawful itemized deductions for damages beyond ordinary wear and tear.

7. LANDLORD ENTRY
Landlord or Landlord's designated agents shall have the right to enter the Premises for inspection or repairs only after providing Tenant with at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice, except in cases of immediate emergency.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with local municipal residential leasing standards.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the date first written above.

Landlord: Apex Property Holdings
Tenant: Jane Doe
