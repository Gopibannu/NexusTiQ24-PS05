COMMERCIAL-RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is executed on February 10, 2024, between Crestview Realty Partners ("Landlord") and Michael Vance ("Tenant").

1. RENTAL PROPERTY
The Landlord leases to Tenant the premises at 450 Oak Ridge Lane, Unit 302.

2. LEASE TERM AND RENT
The lease term is 12 months beginning March 1, 2024. Base rent is fixed at $2,500.00 per month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT REQUIREMENT
Tenant agrees to pay a security deposit equal to three and a half (3.5) months' monthly rent prior to occupancy, totaling $8,750.00, due to high custom interior furnishings provided in the premises.

4. NOTICE PERIOD
Tenant or Landlord must provide forty-five (45) days written notice prior to lease expiration to terminate or elect not to renew the lease.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Landlord shall be responsible for major building repairs, roof, HVAC, and plumbing infrastructure. Tenant shall handle minor interior cleaning and routine care.

6. DEPOSIT DISBURSEMENT
Landlord agrees to return the security deposit within twenty-five (25) calendar days following move-out inspection, subject to allowable wear and tear deductions.

7. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
Landlord agrees to give 24 hours written notice before entering the property for routine maintenance.

Signed on February 10, 2024.

Landlord: Crestview Realty Partners
Tenant: Michael Vance
