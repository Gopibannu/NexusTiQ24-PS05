RESIDENTIAL LEASE CONTRACT

1. IDENTIFICATION OF PARTIES
Landlord: Gotham Real Estate Corp. Tenant: Alex Mercer.

2. PROPERTY & TERM
Unit 7B at 300 Grand Avenue. Lease term begins May 1, 2024, for 12 months. Rent is $2,400.00/month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall deposit $3,600.00 (1.5 months rent) as security prior to occupancy.

4. MAINTENANCE OBLIGATIONS
Landlord maintains central heating, plumbing, roof, and appliances. Tenant maintains interior cleanliness.

5. DEPOSIT REFUND TIMELINE
Landlord agrees to refund the security deposit within thirty (30) calendar days after lease end.

6. AUTOMATIC LEASE RENEWAL
Clause 18. Automatic Renewal: Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Lease Agreement shall automatically renew for an additional full term of twelve (12) months at a 10% rent increase, without requiring any notice or re-execution by either party, and Tenant shall have no right to cancel once the initial term expires.

7. LANDLORD ACCESS
Landlord shall provide 24 hours notice prior to entering for maintenance.
