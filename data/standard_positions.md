# Nexus Property Management — Standard Lease Positions & Policy Rules

**Policy Reference ID**: NPM-STD-2024  
**Version**: 2.4  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2024  

---

## 1. Acceptable Security Deposit Range
- **Standard Rule**: The required security deposit must be between **1.0 month** and **2.0 months** of base monthly rent.
- **Deviation Threshold**: Any deposit requirement less than 1.0 month or exceeding 2.0 months of base monthly rent is an **unacceptable deviation** that requires renegotiation or human legal review.

## 2. Acceptable Termination Notice Period
- **Standard Rule**: Written notice for non-renewal or lease termination must be between **30 days** and **60 days**.
- **Deviation Threshold**: Any notice period shorter than 30 days or longer than 60 days is an **unacceptable deviation**. Shorter notice leaves insufficient time for tenant transition or property turnover; longer notice imposes an unreasonable advance commitment on tenants.

## 3. Required Protection Clause: Maintenance Responsibility
- **Standard Rule**: The lease agreement MUST explicitly mandate that the Landlord/Property Owner is responsible for major structural repairs, heating, ventilation, air conditioning (HVAC), plumbing, electrical systems, and major appliances provided with the unit. The Tenant's maintenance scope must be limited to routine cleanliness, minor interior upkeep, and damages caused by Tenant negligence.
- **Silence / Missing Protection Rule**: If a lease agreement is entirely silent regarding Landlord maintenance responsibilities, this is flagged as a **Critical Missing Protection**. Silence exposes the Tenant or Management to ambiguous liability for major structural or systemic repairs.

## 4. Required Protection Clause: Return of Security Deposit Timeline
- **Standard Rule**: The lease agreement MUST explicitly state a fixed maximum timeframe for returning the Tenant's security deposit post-termination, which cannot exceed **30 calendar days** following lease end or property surrender. The landlord must provide an itemized written accounting of any lawful deductions.
- **Silence / Missing Protection Rule**: If a lease agreement does not specify a maximum deposit return timeline (or allows more than 30 days), this is flagged as a **Critical Missing Protection**.

## 5. Strictly Forbidden Terms (Zero Tolerance Policy)
The company NEVER accepts leases containing any of the following terms under any circumstances:

1. **Automatic Lease Renewal Without Prior Written Notice**: Any clause that automatically renews the lease for a multi-month or annual term without requiring advance written notice from the landlord or giving the tenant reasonable opportunity to opt out.
2. **Waiver of Right to Withhold Rent or Legal Remedies**: Any clause forcing the tenant to waive their statutory or legal right to withhold rent or terminate the lease if the premises become uninhabitable or severely damaged due to landlord neglect.
3. **Unilateral Rent Increases During Fixed Term**: Any clause granting the landlord sole authority to increase monthly rent during an active fixed-term lease without mutual written amendment.
4. **Landlord Entry Without Advance Notice**: Any clause permitting the landlord or agents to enter the dwelling unit without at least 24 hours prior written or verbal notice (except in bona fide emergency situations involving immediate risk to life or property).
