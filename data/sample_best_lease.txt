MODEL RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (BEST PRACTICE STANDARDS)

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is executed on March 1, 2024, by and between Apex Premier Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and John Smith ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND TERM
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the residential premises located at 100 Oakwood Boulevard, Suite 500, Cityville ("Premises"). The term of this Lease shall be for twelve (12) months, commencing on April 1, 2024, and concluding on March 31, 2025.

2. BASE RENT
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly base rent of $2,200.00, payable in advance on the first (1st) day of each calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Prior to move-in, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $3,300.00, which is equal to one and a half (1.5) months' base rent, as a Security Deposit. The deposit shall be held in an escrow account in accordance with state statutory regulations.

4. NOTICE OF TERMINATION OR NON-RENEWAL
Either Landlord or Tenant may terminate or elect not to renew this Lease at the expiration of the initial term by providing at least thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party.

5. LANDLORD MAINTENANCE OBLIGATIONS
Landlord shall be strictly responsible for maintaining and repairing the structural components of the Premises, including exterior walls, foundation, roofing, central HVAC systems, electrical wiring grids, and plumbing infrastructure in good operating condition. Tenant shall maintain interior cleanliness and report defect notices promptly.

6. RETURN OF SECURITY DEPOSIT TIMELINE
Within twenty-one (21) calendar days following the expiration of the Lease and key surrender by Tenant, Landlord shall refund the Security Deposit alongside a written itemized accounting statement of any lawful deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear.

7. LANDLORD ENTRY NOTICE
Landlord or Landlord's authorized agents may enter the Premises for routine inspection or necessary repairs only after providing Tenant with at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice, except in cases of urgent emergency.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, Landlord and Tenant have signed this Agreement as of the date above.

Landlord: Apex Premier Property Holdings LLC
Tenant: John Smith
