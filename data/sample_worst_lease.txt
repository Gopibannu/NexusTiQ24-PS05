HIGH-RISK NON-COMPLIANT LEASE AGREEMENT (WORST CASE SCENARIO)

This Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made on March 1, 2024, between Malicious Landlord Corp ("Landlord") and Unsuspecting Tenant ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND OCCUPANCY
Landlord leases to Tenant the premises at 666 Risk Avenue, Apartment 13. The initial term is one (1) year beginning April 1, 2024.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT
Tenant agrees to pay base rent of $3,000.00 per month due on the first day of each month.

3. EXCESSIVE SECURITY DEPOSIT
Prior to occupancy, Tenant shall pay Landlord a Security Deposit of $10,500.00, equal to three and a half (3.5) months' rent.

4. SHORT TERMINATION NOTICE PERIOD
Tenant may terminate this agreement at the conclusion of the term by providing fifteen (15) days prior written notice to Landlord.

5. FORBIDDEN AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
Upon expiration of the initial term, this Lease shall automatically renew for an additional full one-year term without notice and without option to cancel, binding Tenant to all financial obligations.

6. FORBIDDEN WAIVER OF TENANT LEGAL REMEDIES
Tenant agrees to waive right to withhold rent or seek legal damages under statutory housing codes regardless of building condition or unaddressed habitability issues.

7. OMISSION OF LANDLORD MAINTENANCE OBLIGATIONS
[Notice: This contract omits landlord maintenance responsibility for HVAC, plumbing, structural repairs, or heating systems.]

8. OMISSION OF DEPOSIT REFUND TIMELINE
[Notice: This contract omits any mandatory deadline or timeframe for returning security deposit funds to tenant post-surrender.]

9. INTERNAL CONTRADICTION ON TERMINATION NOTICE
Clause 4 above states 15 days notice, but Tenant must serve ninety (90) days prior written notice to terminate this lease agreement under general notice rules.

Landlord: Malicious Landlord Corp
Tenant: Unsuspecting Tenant
