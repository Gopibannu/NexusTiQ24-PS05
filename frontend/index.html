    STANDARD POSITIONS POLICY RULEBOOK

    DEPOSIT RANGE POLICY

    1. Acceptable Security Deposit Range

    Security deposit required must be between 1.0 month and 2.0 months of base monthly rent. Deposits exceeding 2.0 months are unacceptable deviations.

    NOTICE PERIOD POLICY

    2. Acceptable Termination Notice Period

    Written notice for lease termination or non-renewal must be between 30 days and 60 days. Periods shorter than 30 days or longer than 60 days are unacceptable deviations.

    REQUIRED PROTECTION

    3. Maintenance Responsibility Clause

    Landlord must be explicitly responsible for structural repairs, heating, plumbing, electrical grids, and central HVAC systems. Silence is a critical missing protection.

    REQUIRED PROTECTION

    4. Return of Security Deposit Timeline

    Security deposit refund with itemized accounting must be returned within a maximum of 30 calendar days post-surrender. Silence is a critical missing protection.

    ZERO TOLERANCE

    5. Strictly Forbidden Terms

    Automatic lease renewal without notice, waiver of tenant right to withhold rent, unilateral mid-term rent increases, and landlord entry without 24-hour advance notice.
